About AI Exam Analyzer

AI Exam Analyzer is an AI-powered tool that studies previous year question papers (PYQs) and identifies repeated questions, frequently asked topics, chapter-wise weightage, and smart study plans designed to help students score higher with less effort.

Why We Built AI Exam Analyzer

Students often struggle with exam preparation because they don't know what to study or which topics carry the most weight. PYQs hold the answers — but manually analyzing them takes hours.

AI Exam Analyzer was created to help students prepare faster, smarter, and more strategically by converting raw question papers into meaningful insights within seconds.

What AI Exam Analyzer Does

  • ✔ Identifies repeated questions across multiple years
  • ✔ Shows frequently asked chapters and important topics
  • ✔ Generates predicted weightage for each chapter
  • ✔ Creates quick 1-day, 3-day and 7-day study plans
  • ✔ Works even with scanned PDFs and images using OCR

Who Can Benefit?

  • 🎓 Engineering & university students
  • 📚 Diploma & polytechnic students
  • 🏫 School students preparing for boards
  • 👨‍🏫 Teachers & coaching institutions

Our Mission

Our mission is to make exam preparation simpler and more predictable for every student. We believe that technology — especially AI — can help students focus on what truly matters instead of wasting time searching for patterns manually.
