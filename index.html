What is AI Exam Analyzer?

AI Exam Analyzer is a smart tool that studies your previous year question papers (PYQs) and shows you the most repeated questions, high-weightage topics and a focused study plan. It is designed for students preparing for engineering, university, diploma and school exams who want to study smarter, not harder.

🎯 Focus on What Actually Comes in Exams

Instead of guessing the syllabus, upload your PYQs and let AI find patterns: most repeated questions, frequently asked chapters and the concepts that carry maximum marks.

📚 Works with Scanned PDFs & Images

Have only photocopies or blurry scans? No problem. AI Exam Analyzer can read scanned PDFs and images using OCR and convert them into clean, analyzable text.

⏱️ Perfect for Last-Minute Revision

Just before exams, quickly upload 3–5 previous year papers and instantly get a condensed revision plan highlighting must-do topics to clear or even top the exam.

Frequently Asked Questions

Do I still need to study the full syllabus?

Yes. AI Exam Analyzer helps you understand what is most important, but it is always better to cover your syllabus properly. Use the tool to prioritize revision, not to completely skip topics.

How many papers should I upload for best results?

We recommend uploading at least 3–5 previous year question papers for the same subject or exam. More papers generally mean better pattern detection and stronger predictions.

Is AI Exam Analyzer free to try?

You can start by uploading your papers and seeing the generated insights. In the future, we may add premium features like saving history, exporting reports and advanced analytics for coaching centers.

Is my data and question papers safe?

Your uploaded papers are used only for analysis and improving your experience. We do not publicly share or sell your data. For more information, check our Privacy Policy and Terms.
