Privacy Policy

Your privacy matters to us. This policy explains how AI Exam Analyzer processes your data when you use our platform.

1. Information We Collect

We collect only the minimum data required to provide our services:

  • Uploaded question papers (PDFs or images)
  • Temporary OCR text extracted from your files
  • Basic usage analytics to improve performance
  • We do not collect personal details like name, email, or account information.

2. How Your Data Is Used

Your uploaded files are used only for generating results such as repeated questions, important topics, and weightage predictions.

  • Files are processed in real-time
  • They are not stored permanently
  • They are deleted automatically after processing
  • Your data is never sold or shared

3. Data Security

We use industry-standard security and encryption to ensure safe processing:

  • Cloudflare security protection
  • Secure API communication
  • No permanent file storage

4. Cookies

We use cookies only to:

  • Improve website performance
  • Remember your cookie preferences
  • Analyze usage trends

You can accept or reject cookies at any time.

5. Third-Party Services

We may use third-party tools for analytics, OCR, or AI processing. These services follow strict security standards.

6. Children’s Privacy

AI Exam Analyzer is safe for students but does not knowingly collect personal data from children.

7. Updates to This Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy to improve clarity or comply with new regulations. The latest update date will be shown at the top.
